# THE-FIRST-DRONE
this is my first drone i am going to build it is a pretty scalable one and i am including quite some resources so it could come in use for manyy people 
Hello, I am Bhavya! I study in grade 11 in Rajasthan, India. Recently, I've come across various articles and videos on the internet of people making very innovative things. Hence, I myself thought to build something complex and step it up a bit. For this I decided to make a Drone – A scalable and versatile platform where I can build cool stuff.
The main agenda here is to create a versatile platform for other innovative projects ,to experiment with vision AI ,to test the capabilities and to what extent I can go with them.
This time specifically I am going to build a drone which will follow my mother's car and give me telemetry of what is happening around the car and add complex features like detecting parking spots.All using vision AI.
 I have been making robotics projects now for 1 year, but this is my first drone because I did not simply want to buy a premade chassis. Hence I have designed it in CAD this time.
As this project is not going to be simple I have divided it into 3 separate phases.
Phase 1: Get the base drone up and running.
This includes most of the requested parts in the list.
It consists of attaching the motors, ESC stack, and radio control. Wiring everything up and making sure everything is working in perfect harmony.
Phase 2: This phase requires the GPS module.
Here I will be attaching an Arduino UNO Q with 4GB RAM (I already have it) with the Flight Crontroller and make it follow the GPS location and also add safety protocols.
Phase 3: This phase is the final one which requires a camera which I have not requested in the list because I would like to do more research on requirements ,compatibility and would like to buy it at my own expense. 
I will be documenting my whole journey on Instagram and YouTube.
In the repository I have included the phase 1 drone CAD design ,the phase 1 wiring diagram , a parts list which includes majorly phase 1 and one phase 2 component i.e. the GPS module. And phase 3 I will require a camera which as mentioned above I will buy it myself after further research.
